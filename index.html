Who We Are

Reliable workmanship. Careful time management. Quality service.

Opuntia Global Inc. is a leading construction management and 100% Zimbabwean-owned company. Our core competencies are residential, commercial and industrial project planning, design, construction, renovations, rehabilitation and maintenance.

Since inception, the company has been involved in construction, renovations and maintenance of residential, commercial and industrial properties across the country. We have a reputation for reliability, workmanship and time management.

Our Vision

To be Africa’s most preferred residential, commercial and industrial construction management company.

Our Mission

To provide effective construction services driven by accountability and innovation, while ensuring excellent and quality services at all times.

Our Values

Performance, safety and community benefit.

Performance Excellence

Opuntia Global Inc. is dedicated to timely project delivery, ensuring that scope requirements are met and that clients’ budget constraints are carefully managed to guarantee satisfaction and value.

Health and Safety

We prioritize health and environmental safety by maintaining robust processes and procedures that support compliance with the highest industry standards and safeguard employees, the public and the environment.

Social Investment

Our Services

Residential, commercial and industrial construction services.

Our integrated services span the project continuum from feasibility studies and concept development to construction management and project delivery.

Building Concept Design and Development

We provide a seamless experience from feasibility studies, concept design development, schematic design and construction documentation to permit applications, construction administration, construction management and project delivery.

Land Grading and Site Clearance

We prepare sites for infrastructure development and construction through vegetation removal, debris disposal, hazardous material mitigation and precise topographic alterations.

Site Access Roads and Culvert Bridges

We design and build access roads and culvert bridges that enhance accessibility, safety and efficiency for construction projects.

Bricklaying and General Construction

Our seasoned bricklayers bring over 10 years of experience in industrial, commercial and residential construction, with expertise in plan interpretation, layout preparation and mortar mixing.

Roofing Services

We install, repair and maintain roofs for residential, commercial and industrial clients, including structural repairs, leak detection, gutter installation, insulation, roof painting and maintenance programs.

Ceilings, Skimming and Built-in Cupboards

We enhance interior finishes through ceiling design and installation, built-in cupboards and wall skimming using natural and synthetic materials and devices.

Electrical and Solar Installations

We provide residential, commercial and industrial electrical and solar installations, repairs, upgrades and maintenance, with lighting design, energy efficiency solutions and electrical safety audits.

Plumbing Installations and Maintenance

Our plumbing team handles installations, repairs, drain cleaning, maintenance, water heater services, pipe replacement, fixture upgrades, consultations and design.

Flooring Solutions

We deliver cemcrete, epoxy and tiling solutions for commercial, residential and industrial projects, combining durability, style and functionality.

Residential, Commercial and Industrial Painting

Our painting services include interior and exterior painting, wallpaper removals and installations, faux finishes, colour consultations, surface preparation and drywall repairs.
